Trust & Safety

Built for neighbors, by neighbors.

Here's how we put your mind at ease when lending to someone on your street.

🏘️

Same Zip Code Only

Only people in your zip code can see and borrow your tools. No strangers from across town — just real neighbors.

📋

Neighbor Agreement

Every user signs a Neighbor Agreement before borrowing anything. Clear expectations from the start.

📸

Condition Tracking

Snap a photo during handoff — before and after. Everyone stays accountable without the awkward conversations.
