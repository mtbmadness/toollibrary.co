Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

The Tool Library ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and who we share it with when you use The Tool Library iOS app or website.

We do not sell your personal information. We never have, and we never will.

Section 1

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Name and email address when you register
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Zip code for neighborhood matching

Tool & Activity Data

  • Tool listings you create (name, category, photos, GPS location)
  • Borrow requests, loan history, and ratings
  • Community posts and messages
  • Handoff photos taken at checkout and return

Location Data

  • GPS coordinates when you add a tool (to place it on the map)
  • General zip code for neighborhood filtering
  • We do not track your location continuously or in the background

Device Data

  • Push notification token (to send borrow request and message alerts)
Section 2

Identity Verification (Optional)

If you choose to earn the Verified Neighbor Badge, we use Stripe Identity, a third-party identity verification service, to confirm your identity.

  • Stripe Identity collects a photo of your government-issued ID and a selfie or short video for biometric comparison
  • This data is processed and stored by Stripe, not by The Tool Library
  • Stripe's Privacy Policy governs the handling of this sensitive data: stripe.com/privacy
  • Identity verification is entirely optional and is not required to use the app
Section 3

How We Use Your Information

  • To connect you with neighbors in your zip code
  • To facilitate tool borrowing and lending
  • To send push notifications about your requests and messages
  • To verify your identity (Stripe Identity, optional, for the Neighbor Badge)
  • To process Pro subscription payments (RevenueCat / Apple)
  • To moderate content and enforce our Terms of Use
Section 4

Information Sharing

We share data only with the following service providers who help us operate the app. We do not sell your data to advertisers or data brokers.

  • Supabase — database and authentication provider (supabase.com/privacy)
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (revenuecat.com/privacy)
  • Stripe — identity verification, optional (stripe.com/privacy)
  • Expo — push notification delivery (expo.dev/privacy)
  • Apple — app distribution and payments (apple.com/privacy)
Section 5

User-Generated Content

Content you post (tool listings, community posts, photos) is visible to other users in your zip code. Profile information is visible to neighbors. Messages are visible only to the two parties in a conversation.

Section 6

Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time directly within the app:

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Tool listings and transaction history may be retained for a brief period for legal compliance, then permanently purged. You may also contact us at support@toollibrary.co for data requests or questions.

Section 7

Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure database practices via Supabase. We regularly review our data handling practices to protect your information.

Section 8

Children's Privacy

The Tool Library is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover that a user is under 18, we will delete their account.

Section 9

Your Rights

  • Access your data through your profile in the app
  • Correct your information in Settings → Edit Profile
  • Delete your account in Settings → Delete Account (immediate)
  • Contact us for any data requests or questions
Section 10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Questions about your privacy?

We're here to help. Reach out to our team directly.
